# Native-speaker review list

Every item below is a term or phrase I'm less than certain about. In the app these show a yellow "Verify" marker. To clear one, fix it in `data/*.json` and delete its `flag` field.

Default dialect assumed: Mexican / Central American, formal *usted*.

## Terms that may differ by region or district
- **Report card** (`gra-01`, `ps-14`, Tips): boleta / libreta / tarjeta de calificaciones. The country assignments are from memory.
- **Birth certificate** (`enr-03`): acta de nacimiento (MX) vs. partida / certificado. Which do Central American families use?
- **Immunization record** (`enr-05`): "cartilla de vacunación" is Mexican. Do US school forms say "registro de vacunas"?
- **Free or reduced lunch** (`enr-13`): "almuerzo" vs. "comida" for Mexican families.
- **School records** (`enr-11`): "expediente escolar" vs. "récord".
- **Tardy** (`att-03`): retardo vs. tardanza.
- **Doctor's note** (`att-07`, Tips): justificante / excusa médica / certificado médico by country.
- **Early dismissal** (`att-10`): salida anticipada vs. salida temprana.
- **To fail** (`gra-04`): reprobar. Is it understood by Caribbean and South American families?
- **Counselor** (`beh-13`): consejero vs. orientador.
- **Disciplinary report** (`beh-05`): reporte vs. informe disciplinario.
- **Standardized test** (`gra-09`): prueba vs. examen estandarizado.
- **Tutoring** (`gra-12`): tutorías vs. clases de refuerzo.
- **Asthma inhaler** (`hea-11`): "la bomba" among Puerto Rican and Dominican families.

## Terms I'm unsure of in tone or wording
- **Truancy** (`att-06`): "ausentismo escolar" sounds technical. Is there a plainer phrase?
- **Detention** (`beh-02`): "detención" can sound like arrest. Alternatives: castigo, tiempo de corrección.
- **Lockdown** (`hea-14`): "cierre de emergencia" is my own coinage. Possibly "encierro de seguridad" or "confinamiento".
- **Repeat the grade** (`gra-11`): is there a gentler phrase?
- **GPA** (`gra-13`): the concept may need an explanation.
- **Home language survey** (`enr-08`): official title varies by district.

## Special education terms
- **IEP** (`iep-02`): "Programa de Educación Individualizada" vs. "Individualizado". Use the district's wording.
- **Learning disability** (`iep-06`): discapacidad vs. dificultad vs. trastorno de aprendizaje.
- **Accommodations** (`iep-07`): adaptaciones vs. acomodaciones.
- **Speech therapy** (`iep-09`): terapia del habla vs. terapia de lenguaje.
- **Parent rights / procedural safeguards** (`iep-11`): "aviso de garantías procesales".
- **English learner** (`iep-13`): estudiante que aprende inglés vs. aprendiz de inglés.

## Phrases
- `ps-04`: "Todo lo que se diga aquí es confidencial" (subjunctive; maybe "se hable aquí").
- `pp-05`: "hacen bullying" is colloquial. A parent may say "lo molestan" or "se burlan de él".

## Also worth a general check

- Gender agreement: the cards use "hijo" throughout. The phrases would need "hija" for girls.
