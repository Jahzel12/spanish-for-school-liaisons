# Native-speaker review

Reviewed by Jahzel (native Spanish speaker) on 2026-11-24. All wording flags are cleared, and no "Verify" markers remain in the app.

Dialect: Mexican / Central American, formal *usted*.

## Changes made in review
- Detention: "el castigo después de clases" (was "la detención...")
- Early dismissal: "la salida temprana" (was "salida anticipada")
- Birth certificate: "el certificado de nacimiento" (was "acta de nacimiento")
- School records: "el récord escolar" (was "expediente escolar")
- Tutoring: "las clases de refuerzo" (was "las tutorías")
- Home language survey: "la encuesta del idioma en casa"
- Accommodations: "las acomodaciones" (was "adaptaciones")
